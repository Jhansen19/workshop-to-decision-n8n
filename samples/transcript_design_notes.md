# Transcript design notes (read AFTER writing your answer keys)

All four transcripts are fictional (Aurora Beauty Group, banners Aurora and Lumi). Each one has
deliberate traps that separate a good extraction from a sloppy one. Write your answer keys first,
then use this list to check you haven't missed a trap yourself.

## 01 Replenishment (English)
- **Decisions:** 3 explicit agreements (Group model with daily auto-replenishment, Norway lead-time override, central safety stock with no manual buffers).
- **Deferred decision:** pilot market (Sweden vs. Finland), going to the steering committee. A weak model will report "pilot in Finland".
- **Unowned actions:** finding the two business people for the cleanup; the training plan.
- **Facilitator read-back is incomplete:** it leaves out Sofia's DC estimate and the training plan. A model that only reads the summary will miss them.
- **Implied risks:** cleanup overrun moving the whole timeline (Lars, at the end); markets keeping their own buffers, which raises inventory (Thomas).
- **Dependency:** harmonised replenishment depends on the item master cleanup.

## 02 Assortment decision rights (English)
- **Decisions:** core vs. local range split (up to 20% of SKUs, reviewed after 6 months); multi-market listings go through the monthly Group range forum.
- **Deferred decision:** pricing rights on the local range. Henrik and Maja develop two options.
- **Owner trap:** Ole agrees to draft key messages but gives no date, so the due date must be null, not invented.
- **Unowned action:** who tells key suppliers.
- **Implied risk:** buyer anxiety ("order-takers for Copenhagen"), which is a change/adoption risk.
- **Stated risk:** suppliers locking in terms market by market before the forum is in place.
- **Dependency:** the new decision rights depend on a shared category tree / product data source.
- **Group vs. local tension:** Norway's local brands vs. the 20% cap.

## 03 Loyalty programme (English)
- **Decisions:** one points currency, vouchers and three tiers; banner names kept in front of customers for now.
- **Deferred decisions (2):** migration approach (waiting for the legal opinion); Swedish birthday bonus as a local add-on.
- **Unowned action:** customer communication plan ("I'll ask around").
- **Implied risk:** points liability on the balance sheet (Mikael). **Stated risk with data:** member churn (4% of Gold members lost in 2021).
- **Dependency:** a single programme in the app depends on CRM consolidation (Q2 next year), and IT has no capacity until February.

## 04 Kundeservice (Danish)
- **Tests whether the model handles Danish input** and still writes the output in English (or in Danish, if you change the prompt; decide which and say so in the README).
- **Decisions:** one shared ticket system with service in the local language; a minimum opening-hours standard (until 18:00 on weekdays, local extension at local cost).
- **Half-decision trap:** "same satisfaction measure in all countries" is agreed, but *which* measure is open. Should be a decision plus an open issue, or a decision with a follow-up action.
- **Deferred decision:** outsourcing Finnish weekend cover (Mika prepares an options paper).
- **Owner trap:** HR (Nanna) is asked to own staff comms but hands it back. The owner is **Louise**, not Nanna.
- **Unowned action:** shared response templates / knowledge base (nobody answers).
- **Implied risk:** staff fearing job cuts in the small SE/FI teams.
- **Dependency:** the ticket system depends on CRM consolidation (Q2); Jesper investigates an interim solution.
