Workshop: One Nordic Loyalty Programme – Design Session 1
Company: Aurora Beauty Group (fictional) – banners Aurora (DK) and Lumi (SE/NO/FI)
Date: 23 October 2026, 10:00–12:30, Copenhagen hub + Teams
Attendees: Camilla Brandt (Group CRM Director), Johan Bergström (Lumi Club Manager, SE),
Signe Lund (Group Legal, Privacy), Mikael Nieminen (Group Finance), Pekka Laine (Group Digital
& App), Trine Vestergaard (Aurora Plus Manager, DK), facilitator
Notes taken by: facilitator

---

Facilitator: Goal for today is to agree the backbone of a single loyalty programme. We have
two today: Aurora Plus in Denmark, about 1.9 million members, and Lumi Club in Sweden, Norway
and Finland, about 1.4 million. Camilla, over to you.

Camilla: Thanks. The two programmes work quite differently. Aurora Plus gives points that turn
into vouchers. Lumi Club has tiers, Silver, Gold and Platinum, with tier benefits but fewer
points. Customers who shop in both countries, and there are more of them than we thought, have
two memberships and two apps. The ambition is one programme, one app, one points currency.

Johan: Lumi Club members really value the tiers. If we take Platinum away, we will hear about
it on social media within the hour.

Trine: And Aurora Plus members don't understand tiers at all, they want vouchers.

Camilla: So the question is whether we can have both.

Johan: We could have points that become vouchers, plus tiers on top.

Trine: Three tiers, not four. Simpler.

Camilla: Let me propose: one points currency across all four markets, points convert to
vouchers, and three tiers on top. Can we agree that as the backbone?

Johan: Yes, if the tier benefits are good enough.

Trine: Yes.

Camilla: Agreed then. One points currency, vouchers, three tiers.

Facilitator: Next is the brand. Do customers see one name?

Camilla: My proposal: for now, the programme keeps the banner names in front of customers.
Aurora Plus in Denmark, Lumi Club in the other three. One programme behind the scenes. We revisit
the name when we've decided the banner strategy.

Johan: Good, that protects us in the short term.

(general agreement)

Facilitator: Next, migration. How do existing members move?

Pekka: The simplest technically is automatic migration. Everyone moves over on a set date.

Signe: I need to stop us there. Automatic migration might need fresh consent, depending on how
the original consents were worded. The Lumi Club consents from before 2022 are quite narrow. I
can't say today whether auto-migration is OK.

Camilla: What would you need?

Signe: I need to review both consent texts and the privacy notices, and probably get an external
opinion for Sweden. End of November at the earliest.

Camilla: OK. Then migration approach stays open until Signe has advised. Can you own that?

Signe: Yes, legal opinion by the 30th of November.

Facilitator: Points conversion. How many Lumi points equal one Aurora point?

Mikael: This is where I need to be careful. Whatever rate we choose changes the points liability
on the balance sheet. If we're generous, we're basically writing a cheque. If we're stingy,
members feel robbed. I need to model it before anyone promises a rate.

Johan: Members will compare. If someone had 3,000 Lumi points and now has 1,200 of something
else, they'll think we took their points.

Camilla: Mikael, can you model three conversion options?

Mikael: Yes, for the next session.

Johan: And we'll lose members if we get this wrong. In 2021 when we changed the Gold threshold
we lost about 4 percent of active Gold members in two months.

Camilla: Good data point. Let's note that.

Johan: Can I raise a Swedish thing? The birthday bonus. Swedish members get double points in
their birthday month. It's the single most used benefit in Lumi Club. If we take it away in
Sweden, that's a problem.

Trine: Denmark doesn't have it, and it's expensive.

Camilla: Could it be a local add-on, just in Sweden?

Mikael: Technically yes, but every local add-on makes the programme harder to run.

Camilla: Let's not decide that now. Johan, can you pull the usage data on the birthday bonus?

Johan: Sure.

Pekka: Before any of this launches, the app needs to change. Today Lumi Club runs on a different
CRM platform. The one-app vision depends on the CRM consolidation, which is planned for Q2 next
year. We cannot launch a single programme in the app before that.

Camilla: So realistically launch is after Q2.

Pekka: At the earliest. And my team is already full until February.

Camilla: Understood. We also need a customer communication plan. People will notice.

Trine: Definitely. And store staff need to be able to explain it.

Camilla: Let's find someone to own comms. Probably marketing, I'll ask around.

Facilitator: Thank you all. I'll send the notes.

[End of notes]
