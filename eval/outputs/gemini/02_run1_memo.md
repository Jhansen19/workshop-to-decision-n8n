## Key risks & dependencies
1. **Supplier fragmentation:** Suppliers may attempt to secure favorable terms with individual markets before the Group range forum is fully operational.
2. **Technical execution:** Inability to execute decision rights due to fragmented PIM systems and data silos.
3. **Internal resistance:** Potential anxiety among local buyers regarding loss of autonomy and job security.
