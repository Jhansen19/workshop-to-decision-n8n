## Bottom line  
The Group will own the core product range (≈80 % of SKUs) while each market may own up to 20 % as a local range, with a six‑month review. The next workshop on 4 Nov 2026 must approve pricing options for the local range and the draft RACI. The biggest risk is supplier attempts to secure better terms with individual markets before the Group range forum is operational.

2. **Pricing methodology for the local range** – Options: full group pricing, margin floor set by group, or a hybrid approach. *Recommended next step:* Escalate to Group Pricing (Henrik Dahl) to approve the approach before the 4 Nov session.  
3. **Full PIM integration** – Options: start with a shared reporting view now or merge PIM systems next year. *Recommended next step:* Escalate to IT Leadership to confirm timeline and resources.  
4. **Buyer‑briefing draft deadline** – Options: deliver before the next workshop (4 Nov) or within two weeks of today. *Recommended next step:* Escalate to Ole Madsen to commit to the earlier deadline.  
5. **Supplier communication of centralisation plan** – Options: Group‑level communication led by Karin or market‑level communication. *Recommended next step:* Escalate to Karin Ekström to define the communication lead.
